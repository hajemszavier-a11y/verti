What is BPPV?

Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo is the most common cause of vertigo. Tiny calcium crystals in your inner ear become dislodged, sending false signals to your brain about head position.

The result? Intense spinning sensations triggered by simple movements like rolling over in bed or looking up. The good news: it's treatable with the Epley maneuver, a series of head positions that guide the crystals back where they belong.
