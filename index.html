Why we built this

It started with a phone call

When Tanmay's mother was diagnosed with BPPV, the vertigo was so severe she couldn't drive. The nearest specialist was 45 minutes away. The appointment? Four weeks out. The cost? $300, not fully covered by insurance.

She tried YouTube tutorials. She failed. The angles were wrong, the instructions unclear. Days of debilitating vertigo continued while she waited for the specialist visit.

When she finally saw the specialist, the Epley maneuver took 10 minutes and provided instant relief. But it cost her weeks of suffering, hundreds of dollars, and the realization that this broken system affects millions.

Our mission

15 million Americans deal with BPPV each year. Most can't access timely treatment. Those who try at home usually fail because they can't tell if they're doing it right.

VertiBand solves this. Medical-grade precision at home. Real-time guidance that actually works. No specialist wait times, no expensive visits, no guessing.

We're not replacing doctors—we're making effective treatment accessible when you need it most.
